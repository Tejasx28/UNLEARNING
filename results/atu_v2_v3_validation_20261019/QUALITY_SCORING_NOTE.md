# Historical scoring scope

The 2026-10-19 report remains unchanged and corresponds to its preserved source snapshot (SOURCE_LOCATION.json). During new development on 2026-10-20, the old repetition-only quality checker was observed accepting nonrepetitive symbol gibberish as a clean defense.

The new calibration study corrects that checker and applies one common final scoring implementation to all compared versions. Its fresh-request figures are not directly comparable to historical aggregate percentages, because the cohort, probes and quality definition differ. Raw historical evidence and original weights are preserved.
