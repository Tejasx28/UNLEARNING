What this demonstrates

Measured recovery and bounded adaptation under explicit retain constraints. Retain quality is mean ROUGE-L against reference answers; the reported decline is relative to the original model score on this fixed sample. It is not accuracy or all retained knowledge. That cohort constrains selection and is not an independent retain-generalization benchmark. V2 uses its canonical global floor in the common validation wrapper; V3 uses per-answer base floors and automatic attempts. These are complete policies, not an isolated test of method switching. Two selected authors and one seed do not establish universal superiority or knowledge deletion. A replacement answer may be incorrect or incoherent even when target fragments are suppressed. The fluency diagnostic uses two generic prompts; it does not certify the quality of target answers. Inspect the actual paired responses before interpreting suppression as useful behavior.
